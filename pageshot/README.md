# PageShot

**Capture entire long webpages and responsive viewport screenshots without manually fighting Chrome.**

## Why it exists

Full-page and responsive screenshots are useful during web development, but doing them manually is repetitive. PageShot turns the common capture jobs into three buttons.

## What it does

**Active Tab**  
Captures the entire active Chrome tab at its current viewport.

**Quick Test**  
Captures:

- 430 × 900
- 1440 × 900

**Full Responsive Test**  
Captures eight representative viewports:

- 320 × 568
- 390 × 844
- 430 × 900
- 768 × 1024
- 1024 × 768
- 1280 × 800
- 1440 × 900
- 1920 × 1080

Images are saved to the macOS Desktop.
